ShopOps is an AI-powered operations system connected directly to your Shopify store. Instead of clicking through the admin for every task, you talk to an AI agent in plain English — and it does the work for you.

It can handle 79 tasks across products, orders, inventory, customers, discounts, and more. You do not need to be technical to use it.

🔑

The Two Things You Need

01 — Claude

The AI you talk to. Available at claude.ai. Your agency will set up your account.

02 — ShopOps Plugin

Pre-connected to your store by your agency. Gives Claude the ability to act on Shopify.

Your agency (boco.agency) handles all setup. You do not need to install anything on your computer.

⚡

How It Works

You type a request
→
Claude reads the right skill
→
ShopOps acts on your store
→
Claude reports back

Think of ShopOps as Claude's hands inside your Shopify store.

1

Getting Access

Your agency will provide:

  • Your ShopOps URL (e.g. https://your-store-shopops.duckdns.org)
  • A Claude account or seat at claude.ai
  • Confirmation that your store is connected and live
2

Your First Conversation

Open Claude and start with this exact phrase:

"You are connected to my Shopify store via ShopOps. What can you help me with?"

Claude will respond with a list of everything it can do for your store.

3

How to Ask for Things

You do not need to learn commands. Just describe what you want in plain English.

📦 Products

"Show me all products that are out of stock"
"Update the price of [Product Name] to $49.99"
"Create a new product called Linen Tote Bag, price $35, in the Bags collection"

🛒 Orders

"Show me all unfulfilled orders from today"
"How many orders came in this week?"
"Flag order #1042 — customer says item arrived damaged"

📊 Inventory

"Which products have less than 5 units left?"
"Set stock for [Product Name] to 50 units"
"Give me a low stock report for all variants"

👥 Customers

"Find customer with email john@example.com"
"How many new customers signed up this month?"
"Tag all customers who spent over $500 as VIP"

🏷️ Discounts

"Create a 20% off discount code called WELCOME20"
"Turn off all expired discount codes"
"What discount codes are currently active?"

🗂️ Collections

"Add [Product Name] to the Summer Sale collection"
"Show me everything in the New Arrivals collection"
4

Safety Rules — Built In Automatically

You do not need to manage these. They are built in to protect your store.

👁️

Preview Before Changing

Say "preview this first" and Claude shows exactly what will change before doing it.

✋

Deletes Require Confirmation

Claude will never delete or cancel anything without showing you a summary and waiting for your yes.

📋

Every Change Is Logged

Every action is recorded in an audit log. Your agency can see exactly what happened and when.

↩️

Undo Is Available

Every change creates a snapshot. If something goes wrong, your agency can revert it.

7

What Not to Do

  • ✕Do not share your ShopOps URL publicly — it is connected directly to your live store
  • ✕Do not bulk delete without previewing first — always say "preview first" before any mass action
  • ✕Do not bypass the confirmation step — if Claude asks you to confirm before deleting, that is intentional
  • ✕Do not use on a live store for the first time without testing — ask your agency to test on a dev store first

🔧

Troubleshooting

Problem What to Do
Claude says it can't connect to the store Contact your agency — the connection may need refreshing
A skill ran but nothing changed Check the audit log — ask your agency to review
Claude doesn't understand your request Be more specific: "Update price of SKU-001 to $29"
You want to undo a change Stop using that item and contact your agency immediately
Not sure if an action is safe Add "preview first" or "dry run" before confirming
